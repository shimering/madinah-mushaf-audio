## Recovered opening

The opening of 4:1 was recovered from the older public upload
[فيض الشعراوي — تكريم المرأة / الآية 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3AyaD8F2538).
The first 1072.08 seconds precede the supplied recording. The remainder of that
archival compilation repeats yt-001 and is explicitly excluded.

A genuine introductory topic, «دلالة اسم سورة النساء وتكريم المرأة», spans
3.15–266.795 seconds. Ayah 4:1 combines 266.795–1072.08 seconds of the recovered
source with the supplied continuation. The numbered official-channel upload
BsES3HXJoBA and the older sharawe copy eRloMoeUQdM both start at the later point;
the official upload labeled “episode 12 / Nisa part one” actually repeats the
late-surah lesson. Candidate transcripts and search evidence were preserved.

All 176 ayah entries are available; no missing or partial ayahs are declared in
this corrected edition. This is transcript-context review, with complete human
listening review still pending.

## Provenance and review

Source URLs, original filenames, hashes and recording durations are retained in
`manifest.json`. These are the existing supplied MP3 recordings, cut and joined
with FFmpeg stream copy. The recovered Opus source was encoded once to match the supplied 96 kbps, 48 kHz mono MP3 format. Existing MP3s were not re-encoded; no denoising or loudness processing was applied.

Complete local Arabic transcripts were reviewed for context, repeated quotations,
transitions, recaps and returns to earlier verses. Cuts use preceding inter-word
pauses. Human listening review of the entire edition remains pending; status is
`transcript_context_reviewed_listening_review_pending`.

Source attribution is provenance and does not constitute a redistribution license.
Detailed transcripts and local paths remain in local verification evidence.

`an-nisa-playlist.json` is the authoritative chronological playlist. The M3U8 uses
VLC start/stop options; generic M3U players may ignore these and replay whole files.
